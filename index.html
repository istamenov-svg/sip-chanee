The Founder
“I traded in my mic for a quest to craft the best rosé I could.”

After a decade on the sidelines for the Panthers and the Jaguars, Kavita Channe spent two years in Provence tasting with more than a hundred winemakers before choosing one — a family domaine twenty-five miles from Saint-Tropez, where the winemaker, Thomas, has spent his whole life.

Everything in the bottle follows from that decision.
